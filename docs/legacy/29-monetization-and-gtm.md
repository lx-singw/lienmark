# 29. Monetization & Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

*This document outlines Lienmark's B2B SaaS monetization model, pricing strategy, customer acquisition channels, and strategic partnership expansion roadmap.*

---

## 💰 1. Monetization & Business Model

Lienmark operates on a **hybrid B2B SaaS + Usage-Based Transactional** pricing structure tailored for independent filmmakers, production companies, and major Hollywood studios.

```
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
       |                  Lienmark Revenue Engine                     |
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
       |  1. Transactional Script Audits ($500 - $2,500 / script)    |
       |  2. Enterprise Studio Subscriptions ($25k - $100k / year)   |
       |  3. E&O Insurance Policy Commission (1.5% - 3.0% of binder)   |
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1.1 Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Target Customer | Pricing | Included Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Indie Pass** | Independent Films (<$5M Budget) | **$500 / script run** | - 1 Script Audit Pass<br>- Standard Parallel Search API Verification<br>- Basic PDF Clearance Report |
| **Production Pro** | Mid-Tier Studios ($5M–$50M Budget) | **$2,500 / script run** | - Unlimited Script Re-evaluations (Delta Diffing)<br>- Multimodal Visual IP & Logo Detection<br>- FCP XML / DaVinci EDL Timeline Conformer<br>- Form E&O-2026 Audit Certificate |
| **Studio Enterprise** | Major Studios (A24, Netflix, Universal) | **$50,000 / year** (Unlimited) | - All 32 Bounded Autonomy Capabilities<br>- Multi-Tenant Studio Policy Engine<br>- E&O Insurance Partner Webhook API<br>- Dedicated SLA & Custom Compliance Rules |

> [!NOTE]
> **Product Positioning & Customer Targeting**: Per `03-post-mvp-scope.md` §3, cash-poor indie filmmakers are not the primary enterprise target customer. The $500 Indie Pass serves as an automated, zero-touch self-serve top-of-funnel intake channel, while core recurring revenue relies on Production Pro ($2,500/run) and Studio Enterprise ($50k/yr) partnerships with completion bond companies and E&O insurance underwriters.

## 🚀 2. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

### Phase 1: Entertainment Law Firm & E&O Broker Channel Partners (Months 1–6)
- Partner directly with boutique entertainment law firms (e.g. Ziffren Brittenham, Bloom Hergott) and E&O insurance brokers (Hiscox, Chubb, Front Row Insurance).
- Law firms use Lienmark as a force-multiplier for their paralegals, cutting initial research time from 20 hours to 15 seconds per script.

### Phase 2: Post-Production Software Ecosystem Integrations (Months 6–12)
- Integrate directly into industry-standard screenwriting and post-production software:
  - **Final Draft**: In-editor "Check Clearance Risk" plugin.
  - **DaVinci Resolve / Frame.io**: Timeline marker export for uncleared visual IP.

### Phase 3: Enterprise Title Insurance Platform (Year 2+)
- Expand beyond entertainment into commercial real estate title insurance, corporate M&A IP due diligence, and synthetic media provenance licensing.
