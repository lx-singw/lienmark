## The one-sentence version per competitor, worth having ready verbatim

- **Vitrina:** "Helps you find and vet deal partners. Doesn't tell you if a specific claim in your script is actually cleared."
- **Filmustage:** "Tells you what a scene will cost to produce. Doesn't tell you if you're legally allowed to use what's in it."
- **MUSO/Corsearch/AiPlex:** "Catches people stealing *your* content after release. Doesn't help you clear content *you're* using before release."
- **CAMB.AI/Rask/Dubformer:** "Solves a completely different problem (localization), included here only to show the adjacent-category landscape is genuinely saturated, not to suggest any direct overlap."

## Why this table is honest, not cherry-picked

Worth being self-aware about the risk of a comparison table like this reading as biased by construction — every row was chosen to favor Lienmark's category. That's true, and the honest defense is: **these rows are the actual dimensions a completion bond company or E&O insurer would care about when evaluating whether to trust a clearance verdict** (live verification, sourcing, auditability) — they're not arbitrary categories invented to make competitors look bad. If a judge pushes on this, the honest answer is that this table specifically evaluates rights-clearance-verification capability, not overall product quality — Vitrina and Filmustage are both genuinely strong, well-resourced products at what *they* do; they simply don't do what Lienmark does, and the table says exactly that rather than claiming they're inferior in general.
